\section*{Objective}

We aimed to estimate whether a selective-prediction policy frozen at a source
hospital (MIMIC-CXR-JPG) preserves selective error and coverage at an external
hospital (CheXpert Plus). We evaluated this for image-only, text-only and two
multimodal chest-radiograph models, with pre-diagnostic context intact, removed
or misaligned. We also aimed to determine what the cross-site estimate measures
when labels are derived automatically from reports, and how stable within-site
context effects are.
