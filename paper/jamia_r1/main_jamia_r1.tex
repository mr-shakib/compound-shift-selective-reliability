\section*{Abstract}
% ABSTRACT-START
\noindent\textbf{Objective:} To test whether a selective-prediction policy for
chest radiographs, frozen at one hospital, keeps its error and coverage at
another when pre-diagnostic clinical context is intact, removed or misaligned,
and to establish what the cross-site estimate measures.

\noindent\textbf{Materials and Methods:} Image-only, text-only, late-fusion and
feature-fusion models were trained on MIMIC-CXR-JPG. Per-pathology thresholds
and an 80\%-coverage abstention cutoff were selected at the source and applied
unchanged to CheXpert Plus. Labels for five pathologies came from CheXbert
applied to report impressions. The protocol was registered before data access;
a post-results audit corrected a verified defect, and later analyses are
labelled post hoc.

\noindent\textbf{Results:} The original estimator counted studies without a
labelled target (\NoLabSrcImpression{} at the source, \NoLabExtImpression{}
externally) as error-free, producing transfer gaps of \HOneMThreeOrigPt{} (late
fusion) and \HOneMFourOrigPt{} (feature fusion). Over studies with a labelled
target, the gaps were \HOneMThreeEvalPt{} and \HOneMFourEvalPt{}, with intervals
inside $\pm$0.02. With predictions and thresholds fixed, label handling moved
the feature-fusion gap from \SensUncPosHOneMFourPt{} to \SensUnmNegHOneMFourPt{},
and a second training seed shifted both multimodal gaps by about $-$0.04. Frozen
thresholds moved operating points (cardiomegaly specificity \MFourCardSpecSrc{}
versus \MFourCardSpecExt{}), while coverage stayed within 0.05 of target.

\noindent\textbf{Discussion:} The originally reported transport failure reflected label
availability, not model error. Misaligned context was worse than absent context
for late fusion at the source under every specification, but not externally
under the second seed.

\noindent\textbf{Conclusion:} With report-derived labels available at
different rates, cross-site selective-risk comparisons are not identified.

\begin{figure}[H]
\caption{Transfer gap (H1) for each model under 15 specifications. Squares use
the original estimator. Label-handling and aggregation rows share predictions,
thresholds and cutoffs; coverage rows change the cutoff, $T$ rows the cohort,
findings rows the labels, cohort and thresholds, and seed rows the models.
Dashed lines mark $\pm$0.02.}
\label{fig:spec}
\alttext{Four panels, one per model, each a forest plot of the external-minus-source
selective-error difference with 95\% intervals for 15 analysis
specifications. Original-estimator points lie well to the right of zero for the
primary labels and well to the left for findings labels. Corrected estimates
cluster near zero but range from about $-0.05$ to $+0.13$ across label handling
and training seed.}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]
\caption{M4 sensitivity and specificity among accepted studies at the source
(circles) and external site (squares), labelled cells only.}
\label{fig:operating}
\alttext{Dot plot with one row per pathology and paired source and external
markers, for sensitivity (left) and specificity (right). Sensitivity changes
less than specificity. Specificity drops markedly at the external site for cardiomegaly, edema
and pleural effusion.}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]
\caption{Risk--coverage curves over evaluable studies (C0) at each site,
ranked by the frozen confidence score. Markers show the frozen operating point.}
\label{fig:rc}
\alttext{Two line charts, source and external, of selective error against
coverage for four models. Error rises with coverage for every model; feature
fusion is lowest and the text-only model highest at both sites. Operating-point
markers sit near 80\% coverage.}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[H]

\alttext{Two forest-plot panels, source and external, with paired intervals for

label handling.}
\end{figure}
