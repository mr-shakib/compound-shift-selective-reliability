\section{Objective}
\label{sec:objective}

% JAMIA requires an Objective section matching the abstract heading. The
% venue-neutral draft states the aim across the closing paragraphs of the
% introduction instead; this section states it once, explicitly, and names the
% four prespecified contrasts so the Results section can be read against them.

We asked whether a selective-prediction policy survives deployment at a hospital
other than the one that produced it, when the pre-diagnostic clinical context
the model consumes changes at the same time. The policy under test is the one
that would actually ship: per-pathology decision thresholds and an abstention
cutoff selected once at the source site and transferred unchanged, rather than
re-fitted at the target site.

Four contrasts were prespecified in a hash-manifested protocol before any
external-site result existed:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H1 --- transport.} Selective error at the external site minus
    selective error at the source site, with context intact. Tests whether the
    frozen policy holds its operating point across institutions.
  \item \textbf{H2 --- compound interaction.} The context effect at the
    external site minus the same effect at the source site. A positive value
    would indicate that the two shifts compound super-additively.
  \item \textbf{H3 --- multimodal exposure.} The transfer gap of each
    multimodal model minus the transfer gap of the image-only control. A
    positive value would indicate that consuming clinical context increases
    exposure to institutional transfer.
  \item \textbf{H4 --- conflicting versus absent context.} Selective error
    under replaced context minus selective error under removed context, within
    site. A non-negative value would indicate that misaligned context is no
    better than no context at all.
\end{itemize}

Two further questions were prespecified as mandatory sensitivities rather than
as hypotheses: whether the confirmatory verdicts hold under an alternative
label source derived from a different report section, and whether they hold
under a second training seed. Both are reported with the confirmatory results,
because the first of them changed what the study can claim.
