**Subject: Request for an exemption determination for a secondary analysis of
de-identified, publicly available chest-radiograph datasets**

Dear [Chair],

We request a determination that the study below is exempt from full ethics
review, or the committee's guidance if it is not.

**Study.** "Label availability, not model error, produced an apparent
cross-site failure of frozen selective prediction for multimodal
chest-radiograph models: a prespecified two-hospital evaluation." We evaluate
whether an AI decision policy calibrated at one hospital keeps its error and
referral rates at another. The work is being prepared for the *Journal of the
American Medical Informatics Association*, which asks for an ethics statement.

**Data.** Two public research datasets, used under their data use agreements:
- MIMIC-CXR and MIMIC-CXR-JPG (Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Boston,
  via PhysioNet). Its creation was approved by the BIDMC Institutional Review
  Board, which waived individual consent. Access requires PhysioNet
  credentialing, human-subjects research training and a signed data use
  agreement, which the investigators hold.
- CheXpert Plus (Stanford University, via the Stanford Center for Artificial
  Intelligence in Medicine and Imaging), under its research use agreement.

Both datasets are de-identified by their publishers. We have no means or
intention of re-identifying anyone, no contact with patients, and no access to
any data beyond the published releases.

**Risks.** Minimal: no new data are collected from people, all data are
de-identified, and access is restricted by the publishers' agreements.

We would be grateful for a written determination we can cite in the
manuscript.
